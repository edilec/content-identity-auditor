# Community conduct

Participation should remain professional, specific, and respectful. Discuss
the work rather than the person; assume good faith while asking for evidence;
and do not use harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized
content, doxxing, or publication of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict
participation when conduct harms the project or its contributors. Decisions
will be based on the behavior and available evidence, not a contributor's
status or affiliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[hello@edilec.com](mailto:hello@edilec.com) with the subject **Community conduct:
content-identity-auditor**. Include only the information needed to assess the
report. No response-time or outcome is guaranteed.
